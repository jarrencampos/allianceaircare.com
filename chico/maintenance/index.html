Why Maintenance Matters in Chico

Extend Equipment Life

Chico's extended summer cooling season runs your system hard for months. Regular maintenance keeps all components within spec and adds years to your system's lifespan.

Prevent Costly Breakdowns

A failing component caught during a spring tune-up costs a fraction of an emergency repair at 107°F in July. Maintenance is the most cost-effective HVAC service you can invest in.

Lower Energy Bills

A clean, calibrated system uses less electricity to reach setpoint temperature — savings you see every month on your PG&E bill throughout Chico's demanding cooling season.

Orchard & Almond Dust

Chico is surrounded by almond and walnut orchards. Harvest season sends significant particulate loads through filters and coils. We address this agricultural buildup at every maintenance visit.

Wildfire Smoke Awareness

Chico's proximity to the Sierra Nevada foothills means wildfire smoke is a recurring seasonal concern. We inspect for smoke-related contamination in your system and recommend appropriate filters for smoke events.
