Why Mini Splits Work Great in Chico

No Duct Losses

Mini splits deliver conditioned air directly to each zone — no energy lost through leaky duct runs in your attic during Chico's extreme summer heat when attic temperatures can top 150°F.

Zone Control

Cool only the rooms you're using. Each head unit has its own thermostat so you're not paying to cool empty spaces during Chico's long, 100°F+ afternoon hours.

Year-Round Comfort

Modern mini splits provide efficient heating and cooling in one compact system — handling Chico's hot summers and cooler winters without requiring separate heating equipment.

Older Homes Near CSUC

Many older Chico homes near campus have inadequate or no ductwork for certain rooms. Mini splits add reliable climate control to these spaces without major renovation.

Rebate-Eligible

Many qualifying systems are eligible for PG&E rebates and federal energy efficiency tax credits — ask us about current incentives when you request your free quote.

Quiet Operation

Mini split heads run far quieter than traditional forced-air systems — ideal for home offices, bedrooms, and study spaces where noise is a real concern.
