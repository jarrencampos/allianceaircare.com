Why Ductwork Matters So Much in Chico

Extreme Attic Temperatures

Chico attics regularly hit 145–150°F on summer afternoons. Even small duct leaks into that superheated space lose enormous amounts of energy. What feels like a well-running system can be wasting 20–30% of its output through leaky attic ducts.

Uneven Cooling

Hot spots in specific rooms — especially far from the air handler — typically trace back to duct leaks or undersized branch runs. We identify exactly where airflow is lost and restore proper balance throughout your home.

Older Chico Homes

Many Chico neighborhoods near downtown and the university feature older homes with ductwork installed decades ago. Deteriorated flex duct, failed mastic joints, and collapsed sections are common problems we find and correct.

Agricultural Dust Infiltration

Leaky return ducts in Chico can draw orchard dust and particulates directly into the air distribution system, bypassing the filter. Sealing leaks stops both energy loss and contamination.

Lower Energy Bills

A properly sealed and insulated duct system in a Chico home can reduce cooling costs significantly — particularly valuable during the long, expensive summer cooling season when systems run for months.

Full System Replacement

When existing ductwork is beyond repair — heavily damaged, poorly designed, or simply inadequate — we design and install a complete replacement system sized and balanced correctly for your home.
