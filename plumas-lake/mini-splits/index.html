Why Mini Splits Make Sense in Plumas Lake

No Duct Losses

Mini splits deliver conditioned air directly to each zone — no energy lost to duct leaks in attics or walls. Significantly more efficient than central systems in many applications.

Zone Control

Set the bedroom to 68° while the living room stays at 72°. Each indoor unit operates independently, so you condition only what you're using.

Year-Round Comfort

Modern mini splits handle both hot Plumas Lake summers and cooler winters efficiently — no separate heating system needed in mild climates.

Quiet Operation

Mini split indoor heads run at very low decibel levels. Ideal for bedrooms, home offices, and study rooms where noise is a concern.

Rebate-Eligible

Many high-efficiency mini split models qualify for utility rebates through SMUD or PG&E and federal energy efficiency tax credits to offset upfront costs.

Perfect for Room Additions

Plumas Lake's newer homes often have room additions or converted garages that aren't served by existing ductwork — a mini split is the cleanest, most efficient fix.
